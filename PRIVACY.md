# RUSHplify – privacy

RUSHplify includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting telemetry. Preferences, Stage
layouts, favourites, Test Rig templates, cached catalogue data and restore points stay on the computer.

When connected, RUSHplify talks only to the selected HeadRush device on the local network. TONE3000 and
HeadRush Cloud requests go through the capabilities the pedal itself exposes. A direct TONE3000
connection (OAuth) is planned; it will be described here once it exists.

Avoid sensitive information in rig, setlist and stage names, and review backup files before sharing them.
